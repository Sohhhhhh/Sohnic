# Business Overview

## Problem Statement

### Business Context

Manufacturing and distributing electronic components and development boards through a multi-branch retail network presents significant operational complexities.
The business model involves procurement from external suppliers, production through third-party manufacturing facilities, and distribution across a network of branch locations — each requiring precise coordination and real-time visibility

### Current Challenges

#### Supply Chain Fragmentation

Without an integrated system, tracking raw materials from multiple suppliers through external manufacturing and into distributed storage facilities becomes disconnected and unclear
This makes it difficult for stakeholders to see real-time updates on material availability, production progress, and inventory levels across the network

#### Operational Inefficiencies

Manual processes and disconnected systems result in:

- Delayed decision-making due to lack of real-time data
- Mismatches between actual stock and recorded inventory
- Redundant data entry and increased likelihood of human error

#### Quality Management Gaps

The absence of end-to-end traceability makes it difficult to identify root causes when quality issues arise
Connecting defective products back to specific raw material batches or supplier sources becomes a time-intensive investigative process

#### Financial Opacity

The absence of a unified financial tracking system limits visibility into profitability across branches, products, and suppliers.
This limits the company’s ability to make informed strategic decisions about product offerings, branch performance improvement, and supplier management.

# Business Process Flow

## Phase 1: Raw Material Procurement

1. Main branch procurement team identifies material needs based on:
   - Current inventory levels
   - Production plans
   - Sub-branch demand forecasts
2. Requests for quotations (RFQs) are sent to multiple suppliers outlining:
   - Required materials and quantities
   - Quality and delivery specifications
3. Suppliers submit quotations, which are evaluated based on price, quality, and lead time
4. The procurement team selects the most suitable supplier and issues a purchase order (PO)
5. Raw materials received at main branch warehouse
6. Quality inspection and storage

---

## Phase 2: Manufacturing

1. Main branch prepares material kits for each product type
2. Materials shipped to external manufacturer with:
   - Model specifications
   - Quantity requirements
3. Manufacturer assembles and tests products
4. Finished products returned to main branch warehouse
5. Quality control inspection and functional testing

---

## Phase 3: Distribution

1. Sub-branches submit requests for inventory:
   - Specify product types and quantities needed
   - Based on sales forecasts and current stock
2. Main branch reviews and approves requests
3. Products transferred from main to sub-branch warehouses
4. Transfer documented with delivery notes

---

## Phase 4: Sales

### For Manufactured Products

- Main Branch: Sells directly to customers from main warehouse stock
- Sub-Branches: Sell from their local inventory
- All sales recorded at uniform pricing per product type

### For Base Components & Supplies

- Products procured directly from suppliers
- Stored in warehouse inventory (no manufacturing required)
- Distributed to sub-branches as needed
- Sold directly to customers with standard markup

### Target Customers

- Electronics hobbyists and makers
- Students and educational institutions
- Professional engineers and prototyping labs
- Repair shops and service centers
- Small-scale manufacturers and startups
- DIY enthusiasts

User Story:  
_As a sub-branch manager, I need to request inventory from main branch_

1. Sub-branch creates transfer request:
   - Select products and quantities
   - Add notes
   - Submit for approval
2. Main branch reviews:
   - Check stock availability
   - Approve or reject with comments
3. Upon approval:
   - Generate transfer order
   - Update inventory (deduct from main)
   - Print delivery note
4. Sub-branch confirms receipt (add to sub)

### Transfer Tracking

- Approved/rejected history

- Received confirmations

## F. Quality & Traceability Module

User Story:  
_As an inspector, I need to monitor the quality of both raw materials and finished products, and trace any defects back to their source for corrective action_

Features:

- Raw Material Inspection:

  - Inspect incoming raw materials and components before they enter warehouse stock
  - Record inspection results (pass/fail, defect type, quantity rejected)
  - Link each material batch to its supplier and purchase order
  - Submit a return request to the manager for defective or substandard materials
  - Manager can approve or reject the return request before it’s sent to the supplier
  - Each request has a status (Pending → Approved/Rejected → Completed).

- Manufacturing Output Inspection:

  - Inspect finished product batches received from the manufacturer
  - Record inspection status (approved, rejected, needs rework)
  - Link inspection data to corresponding manufacturing batch and raw material batches used
  - Submit a return request to the manager for defective finished goods
  - Manager reviews and either approves the return to manufacturer or rejects the request
  - Each request has a status (Pending → Approved/Rejected → Completed).

- Secondary Storage Inspection:

  - Inspect finished goods upon arrival at secondary storage
  - Record any damages or discrepancies during transport or handling
  - Submit return request to the manager if major defects or damage are found
  - Each request has a status (Pending → Approved/Rejected → Completed).
